# LadderIQ v3.60.5 — Automatic Candidate Visibility

- Removes the Watch List section from the dashboard.
- Populates Growth Candidates directly from the broad-market score file.
- Displays confirmed candidates at OPS 95+ and emerging candidates at OPS 90–94 or 1/2 confirmation.
- Exact 100 OPS remains the elite tier.
- Emerging candidates are visible but do not receive capital or an active buy ladder until the second qualifying trading session.
- Adds market-scan freshness and candidate counts to the sidebar.
- Supports older cached leadership files that do not contain the new candidate_eligible field.
- Updates the visible dashboard version to 3.60.5.
